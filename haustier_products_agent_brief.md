**Business type:** Manufacturer and exporter.

- The visual language should feel premium, international, editorial, and manufacturing-focused.

## 11. Hero Direction — Initial Concepts To Explore

### Concept A — The Dog / Personality

Use a highly polished 3D dog character or realistic 3D dog as the visual anchor.

Possible interaction:
- Dog subtly follows cursor
- Head/eyes react to cursor
- Collar becomes visually prominent
- Scroll transitions between different dog personalities/breeds
- Collar/accessory remains the product connection

**Strength:**
Creates emotional connection immediately and makes the website memorable.

**Risk:**
Could make the company feel like a consumer pet brand rather than a serious B2B manufacturer if the execution becomes too playful.

**Best version:**
Luxury/editorial dog photography or sophisticated 3D rather than cartoon/mascot aesthetics.

---

### Concept B — Product / Craftsmanship

Make the collar itself the hero object.

Possible sequence:
1. Finished collar appears.
2. Camera moves into the material.
3. Leather grain / stitching / hardware become visible.
4. Collar rotates.
5. Components subtly separate.
6. Materials and construction are revealed.
7. Components return to finished product.
8. Transition into real factory footage.

**Strength:**
Immediately communicates what the company actually manufactures and can feel very premium.

**Risk:**
A conventional product-focused 3D site can become visually similar to other luxury product websites.

---

### Concept C — Material → Product → Factory

Potentially the strongest hybrid direction.

Start with a macro view of leather/material.

Then:
**MATERIAL**
→ **CRAFT**
→ **PRODUCT**
→ **FACTORY**
→ **GLOBAL SUPPLY**

The hero begins abstractly and progressively reveals that the object is a dog collar.

This would make the website feel like a luxury materials/manufacturing brand rather than an online pet shop.

# Agent Brief — One Paragraph

Build a premium, highly visual **B2B manufacturer/exporter website** for HAÚSTIER PRODUCTS, a Kanpur-based manufacturer/exporter of pet products and leather/non-leather accessories. The website is primarily for credibility, international positioning, product/capability presentation, and generating business enquiries — NOT for retail e-commerce. The existing site identifies dog collars, toys, bags and belts, while public industry sources also associate the company with leashes, harnesses, beds, cat products, chew toys and equestrian products; treat anything beyond the current site's explicit categories as unconfirmed until the client provides a catalogue. The company states that its executives have 35+ years of industry experience, combines technology with craftsmanship, and has in-house facilities for product-related production segments. The redesign should feel premium, tactile, editorial and international, with real product/factory imagery supported by carefully chosen 3D/WebGL interactions. Do not invent statistics, certifications, clients, capacities or capabilities. The primary conversion should be a business enquiry / catalogue request / partnership CTA.
